A few things to know

RaxLab helps you plan—it does not predict or guarantee results.

  • Pack results vary. The default 10.0 rating or your custom average is a scenario, not guaranteed pack contents. Plans assume one 200-Rax pack daily with sufficient funding.
  • Earnings are calculated estimates. Your base earnings and the listed tier multiplier determine the result. Real may apply other payout rules or rounding. These are not OTD predictions.
  • Rules may change. The included thresholds and multipliers may become outdated. Verify them in Real before spending.
  • Browser-only storage. Players and settings stay in this browser, not a cloud account. Clearing site data can remove them. Export regular backups; player exports currently do not include global settings.
  • No automatic feedback submission. Copying or downloading a message does not send it. Send it yourself to raxlabsupport@proton.me.
  • Independent project. RaxLab is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Real, any sports league, team, or player.
